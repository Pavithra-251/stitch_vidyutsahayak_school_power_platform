Emergency Guidelines

In case of sparking transformers, sagging high-tension lines, or waterlogged switchboards, keep students 50m away and immediately notify district power dispatchers.

Offline Protocol

Technicians in remote zones can log diagnostic steps via USSD (*999*843#) or sync through local field mesh nodes once radio line-of-sight is established.
